# Idejo Contributor Licence Agreement

This document contains two agreements:

- **Part A — Individual Contributor Licence Agreement**, signed by every person who contributes.
- **Part B — Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement**, signed in addition by an employer or
  other legal entity whose employees or contractors contribute on its behalf.

Both agreements grant licences. **You keep the copyright in your Contributions.** Nothing in
either agreement transfers ownership.

Part C explains how to sign.

## Part C — How to sign

You sign **once**. Your signature covers every Contribution You have made and will make to the
Project; You do not sign again for later pull requests.

### Individuals (Part A)

1. Open a pull request against the Project.
2. The automatic CLA check runs on the pull request. If it has no signature on record for You,
   it marks the pull request as blocked and posts a comment with a link to this document.
3. Read this document, then reply on the pull request with exactly this sentence:

   > I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

4. The check records Your GitHub username, the pull request and the date and time of Your
   signature in the Project's signature record (file `signatures/cla.json` on the
   `cla-signatures` branch), and the pull request is unblocked.

Posting that sentence from Your GitHub account is Your electronic signature of Part A. If the
check does not update, comment `recheck` on the pull request.

If more than one person authored commits in a pull request, each of them must sign before it
can be merged.

### Corporations (Part B)

1. An officer or other person authorised to bind the Corporation completes the details below
   and Schedule A, signs, and sends a copy to the Steward at mariano.pictet@proton.me.
2. The Steward confirms receipt and keeps the signed copy on record.
3. Each person listed in Schedule A also signs Part A once, through the check on their first
   pull request, as described above.
4. To add or remove designated people later, send an updated Schedule A to the Steward.
